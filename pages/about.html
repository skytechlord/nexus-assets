Our Story

NexusAssets started as a personal bookmark folder — a collection of free Blender addons, texture packs, and VFX overlays that one developer kept sharing with friends on Discord. Every time someone asked "where do you get your assets?", it took 10 messages to explain. So we built a website.

What started as a weekend project quickly grew into a real platform as artists from around the world started contributing assets, sharing the site, and requesting new categories. Today, NexusAssets is home to hundreds of free resources across five categories — with more added every week.

💡 Our mission: Make professional-grade creative resources accessible to every artist regardless of budget, location, or experience level.

What We Stand For

🔓

Always Free

Core assets will always be free. We may add optional premium packs in the future, but our base library stays open for everyone.

✅

Quality First

Every asset is reviewed before publishing. We check that files open correctly, are documented, and actually deliver what they promise.

⚖️

Clear Licensing

All assets use CC0 (public domain) or CC-BY licenses. No ambiguous terms — you always know exactly what you can do.

🌍

Community Driven

We accept asset submissions from creators worldwide. If you've made something useful, share it here and help the community grow.
