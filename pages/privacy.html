Privacy Policy

We keep this simple and honest. Here's exactly what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

Home › Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2025  ·  Effective date: 1 June 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to NexusAssets ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how we handle information when you use our website at nexusassets.io (the "Site").

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have regarding your data. By using NexusAssets, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Short version: We currently collect very little data. No accounts, no tracking pixels, no selling your information to third parties. This site is a static resource platform.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

Currently, the only way you voluntarily give us information is through our contact form. If you use it, we may collect:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your email address
  • The subject and content of your message

This information is used solely to respond to your enquiry. We do not add you to mailing lists without your explicit consent.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log standard technical information when you visit, including:

  • Your IP address (anonymised where possible)
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • The website that referred you to us
  • Date and time of your visit

This data is used only for understanding overall traffic patterns and improving the site. It is not linked to any personal identity.

2.3 What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not require accounts or logins (no account data)
  • We do not store payment information (all downloads are free)
  • We do not use third-party advertising trackers
  • We do not use social media login buttons
  • We do not build behavioural profiles of visitors

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the limited information we collect to:

  • Respond to messages sent through our contact form
  • Monitor and improve site performance and user experience
  • Detect and prevent abuse or fraudulent activity
  • Comply with legal obligations if required

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Cookies

NexusAssets currently uses only essential technical cookies that are necessary for the site to function correctly. We do not use marketing cookies, tracking cookies, or analytics cookies from third-party services.

As we grow, we may add an analytics tool (such as a privacy-focused option like Plausible or Fathom). If we do, we will update this policy and add a cookie consent notice.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Doing so will not affect your ability to browse or download assets from this site.

5. Third-Party Services

Our site uses the following external services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Google Fonts — used to load our typefaces (Syne and DM Sans). Google may log font requests. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Unsplash — asset preview images are loaded from Unsplash's CDN. They may log image requests. See Unsplash's Privacy Policy.

We have no control over how these third parties handle data associated with requests to their servers.

6. Data Retention

Contact form messages are retained only as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry — typically no more than 90 days. Automatically logged server data is retained for a maximum of 12 months before being deleted or anonymised.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of data we hold about you
  • Correction — request we correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion — request we delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Objection — object to how we process your data
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at our contact page. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

NexusAssets is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the site grows and new features are added (such as user accounts or analytics). When we make significant changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please reach out:

  • Via our contact form
  • By email: privacy@nexusassets.io
